# About Me

![Profile Picture](./assets/images/profile.png)

## **Who am I**

MS Student Researcher specializing in Autonomous Driving. I bridge the gap between classical Control Theory and modern Data-driven AI to create trustworthy and high-performance autonomous agents.

## **Research & Philosophy**

My research focuses on advanced Behavior Planning using Reinforcement Learning (RL) and End-to-End (E2E) Diffusion models.

- **The Philosophy:** I believe the most reliable autonomous systems stem from a "Simple and Robust" rule-based foundation, synergized with the adaptability of modern AI.
- **The Goal:** To develop robust, safe, and efficient systems that leverage the interpretability of classical logic and the powerful generalization of large-scale data.

## **Current Focus**

- **Sim-to-Real Deployment:** Deploying E2E models from CARLA simulation to Real-world vehicles
- **Hybrid Behavior Planning:** Optimizing planning through IL (Behavior Cloning) + RL (Soft Actor-Critic) using the Waymo Open Dataset

## **Key Expertise**

- **Planning & Learning:** Deep RL (SAC), Imitation Learning, E2E Diffusion Models
- **Control & Robotics:** Rule-based Control Logic, Vehicle Dynamics, Sim-to-Real Deployment
- **Perception:** Camera-based Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion (Camera/LiDAR, OpenCV)
- **Experience:** Full-stack development from autonomous driving competitions to real-vehicle integration
